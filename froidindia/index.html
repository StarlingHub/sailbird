How it works

Very Easy to Use.
Reusable.
Reliable Cooling every time.

1

Soak in tap water for 10 minutes

Submerge the inner black cooling pouch in ordinary tap water. No special preparation, no fridge needed, no ice required. no cold water needed.

2

Towel dry for 25-30 minutes

Remove excess surface water by putting it on towel for 25-30 mins (DO NOT - squeeze or press the pouch to remove excess water.)

3

Place your insulin inside

Fits standard pens and vials. Closes securely with zipper or hook-and-loop.

Stay cool for 50+ hours
